How the cards are made

Every uploaded hand is read in the uploader's browser. For each opponent the page counts what they did (entered the pot, raised first in, 3-bet, folded to a steal, folded to bets on each draw or street, stood pat, showed down). A read appears when a player's rate is far enough from everyone else's on the same chances that it is unlikely to be luck: a z-score of 2.5 or more on at least 25 chances, the top five per format. Preflop numbers pool the blind games; WTSD and fold-to-river pool 2-7 Triple Draw, A-5 Triple Draw, Badugi and Badeucy. "Shown weak" rates only see hands that reached showdown. Style compares a player's VPIP with the pool's and their PFR with their VPIP, on 100+ hands. With a few hundred hands, treat a read as a lean, not a rule.

Players change, so by default recent hands count more: each month's hands count half as much as the month three months later (switch this under Weighting). Hand counts on the cards are always real hands; the percentages and reads use the weighting, and a read still needs 25 real chances.

Notes in gold were written by hand for a few regulars and do not update with new uploads.

The cash record adds up PhenomDB's weekly cash leaderboards since March (profit is after rake; "avg buy-in" is total chips bought in divided by sessions, a rough guide to stakes). The tournament record section comes from PhenomDB, which builds it from public on-chain buy-ins and payouts. It is refreshed daily for players with a card, it only shows players PhenomDB has matched to a name, and players who set their PhenomDB profile to private are left out.
